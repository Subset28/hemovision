Your iPhone becomes a guide. 100% Offline.

OmniSight is a real-time obstacle awareness app that uses your iPhone camera and on-device AI to identify nearby objects and narrate your surroundings through voice — no internet, no cloud, no data ever leaving your device.

Built by a student developer for the Virginia TSA State Conference, where it earned 2nd place in Software Development.

KEY FEATURES

Real-Time Object Detection
A lightweight YOLOv8 model identifies pedestrians, vehicles, furniture, animals, and obstacles in real time across 12 object classes. Runs entirely on-device. No account required.

Spatial Voice Descriptions
Every announcement tells you both what and where — "Car, slightly left, 3.2 meters, approaching." Direction and distance, every time.

Collision Warnings
OmniSight tracks how fast objects approach you and fires priority warnings — "Warning! Car, 0.9 meters!" — before a collision can develop. Emergency announcements bypass the speech queue and play immediately.

LiDAR Obstacle Detection
On iPhone Pro models, LiDAR catches obstacles the camera misses — low furniture, open doors, transparent surfaces — and warns you with "Obstacle ahead, 0.6 meters."

Crosswalk Signal Detection
The camera reads WALK / DON'T WALK signs in real time using on-device OCR and announces the signal the moment it changes.

Crowd Awareness
When 3 or more people surround you, OmniSight announces the crowd count and suppresses per-person clutter so you hear the room, not a list.

Haptic Feedback
Three vibration levels signal urgency — a light tap for mode changes, a medium pulse for crosswalk signals and found objects, a strong burst for collision warnings.

Find Mode
Looking for a specific object? Switch to Find mode, pick a class (chair, door, person, car…), and OmniSight guides you to the nearest one with direction and distance.

Fully Configurable
Adjust speech rate, announcement range, emergency warning distance, scene update frequency, and which object classes to announce — all from the Settings screen.

Complete Privacy
All camera processing happens on-device. OmniSight never connects to the internet. Camera frames are processed in memory and discarded immediately.

REQUIREMENTS

Requires iOS 17.6 or later. LiDAR features require iPhone 12 Pro or newer Pro model.

SAFETY NOTE

OmniSight is an assistive tool, not a replacement for white canes, guide dogs, or orientation and mobility training. Distance readings are estimated. Always use personal judgment when navigating.
