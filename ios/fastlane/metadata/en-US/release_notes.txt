New in 1.1:

• Crosswalk detection — OmniSight now reads WALK / DON'T WALK signals via on-device OCR and announces them instantly
• Crowd awareness — when 3+ people surround you, the app announces the crowd count and suppresses per-person clutter
• Visual bounding boxes — color-coded overlays show HIGH-priority objects (orange) vs normal (white) at a glance
• Haptic differentiation — three vibration levels now signal urgency: light for mode changes, medium for crosswalk/find events, strong for collision warnings
• Find mode — search for a specific object class; the app guides you to it with direction and distance
• Settings expanded — speech rate slider, emergency distance picker, announcement range multiplier, per-class enable/disable toggles
• Safety fix — emergency collision alarms now fire even when an object class is toggled off in Settings
• Safety fix — collision detection now runs in Find mode (was silently skipped)
• Safety fix — LiDAR obstacle threshold now respects your Emergency Warning Distance setting
• Reliability fix — crosswalk detector race condition eliminated; apostrophe variants in "DON'T WALK" handled correctly
